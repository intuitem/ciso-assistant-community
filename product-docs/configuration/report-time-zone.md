# Report time zone

Reports and exports are generated by the server, not by your browser, so their times cannot follow your screen. They are printed in the instance's **report time zone**, which is **UTC** by default. If a time in a report looks one or two hours off from the same time on screen, the report time zone is most likely still UTC.

## Change the report time zone

Changing it requires the permission to change global settings.

1. Go to **Extra → Settings** and open the **General** tab.
2. Open the **Date format** section.
3. Under **Report time zone**, pick your region, for example `Europe/Paris`. Regions are listed from west to east, each with its current UTC offset.
4. Save.

The new zone applies to every report generated afterwards. A report already downloaded keeps the times and the zone printed in it.

## How times are printed

Every time carries its own UTC offset:

| Report time zone | A time printed in a report |
| --- | --- |
| `UTC` (default) | `2026-07-14 06:30:00 (UTC)` |
| `Europe/Paris` | `2026-07-14 08:30:00 (UTC+02:00)` |
| `Asia/Kolkata` | `2026-07-14 12:00:00 (UTC+05:30)` |
| `America/New_York` | `2026-07-14 02:30:00 (UTC-04:00)` |

Each report also states its zone at the top:

```
Generated at   2026-10-23 09:58:11 (UTC+02:00)
Time zone      Europe/Paris - each time shows its UTC offset
```

{% hint style="info" %}
The offset is worked out for each time, because it changes with daylight saving time: in Paris, a time in July shows `(UTC+02:00)` and a time in December shows `(UTC+01:00)`. On the night clocks go back, the same local time can appear twice with two different offsets, for example `02:30:00 (UTC+02:00)` then `02:30:00 (UTC+01:00)`. Both are correct and one hour apart.
{% endhint %}

## What follows the report time zone

- The PDF reports: audit report, attestation, action plan, findings report, incident report and risk report.
- The Markdown exports of incidents and findings assessments.
- The Word audit report.
- The publication and generation dates of policy document PDFs.
- The creation time of each finding in the findings Excel export.

## What does not change

- **Times on screen** keep following your browser.
- **Calendar dates** such as due dates and ETAs are printed as they are, without any conversion.
- **The API and the CSV exports** keep their times in UTC.
- **Your personal date format** only controls how dates are laid out on screen. See [Date format](date-format.md).

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Custom Word templates.** In a [custom template](settings/custom-templates.md), `{{ date }}` is the report date in the report time zone, and `{{ generated_at }}` now reads `2026-10-23 09:58:11 (UTC+02:00)` instead of ending with `UTC`. Check templates that display it.
{% endhint %}

## Renamed regions

Only current region names are offered. If your instance already stores a region that has since been renamed, such as `Europe/Kiev` (now `Europe/Kyiv`), it is kept, still shown in the list and still used; pick the new name whenever convenient.
